How to Play ZenKnot

• Untangle the Threads: Drag crystal nodes around the sacred canvas to untangle intersecting strands.

• The Golden Rule: Non-adjacent threads must NOT cross or overlap. Threads that connect to the same node are natural endpoints and are never crossings.

• Visual Cues: Crossing strands show coral dashed lines with glowing intersection markers. Untangled strands glow solid cyan and emerald.

• Keyboard Controls: Press Tab to cycle nodes, Arrow Keys or WASD to nudge nodes, Z to Undo, H for Hint, R to Restart.
